1.3   The BRIQUETTE PRESS is a hydraulic compactor designed to reduce the volume
      of processing waste.
      Each type of material has its own problems and the dimensions of the pieces of
      waste differ. We can nevertheless confidently state that the solid cylinder or
      briquette that results from compaction makes the storage, handling and recycling
      of the different materials easier and cost-saving.

      The machine consists of a compact metal structure in which the components have
      been positioned so as to take up as little space as possible.
      The electric control panel that has been placed at the front with a switch panel that
      conforms to different EU standards enables the machine to be controlled during the
      different operating phases.
      The base comprises the tank containing non-rerefined column oil.
      A supporting platform houses the motor, mor-pump coupling, filter, pump, valves,
      gauge, pipes and fittings.
      The compression and feed urit on the base is controlled by a series of solenoid
      valves.
      The hydraulically controlled press tongs enable automatic compression of the
      briquette by means of the sleeve.
      A large cylindrical container is fixed in the upper part of the base for storing the
      matenal to be compacted, together with a viewer and optical level probe.
      Inside the base there is a disk wheel controlled by the plunger that rotates to feed
      the compression unit.

      Using certified raw materials makes our machine reliable and provides an effective

      COMPONENTS

      The briquette press is a hydraulic compactor consisting of seven main component
      units:



2.1   SUPPORTING STRUCTURE
      Consists of a base in sheet metal that together with the oil tank supports all the
      other system components.

2.2   FEED BOX
      Consists of a metal slide that runs on a guide moved by a hydraulic cylinder that
      with alternating movement pushes the material into the block by feeding the plunger
      for the next briquette compaction and extrusion phase.

2.3   COMPRESSION UNIT
      Consists of a hydraulic cylinder that pushes the plunger to compact and extrude the
      material to form the briquette.

2.4   PRESS TONGS
      Formed by a sleeve divided into two shells with an internal diameter section of 55
      mm, and hydraulic tongs at the end that alternately compressing the sleeve and
      stop the material exiting during tie plunger-return phase.

2.5   FEED UNIT
      Consists of a container, material tank, for storing material to be compacted and of
      a blade impeller or disk wheel, that rotates by set intervals, is moved by a lever
      located on the plunger and facilitates filling of the feed box with material.

2.6   HYDRAULIC SYSTEM
      Consists of 120-litre oil tank, integrated with the supporting structure, a cast-iron 25
      cm3 gear pump, a set of solenoid valves controlled in sequence by a PLC located
      inside the power panel that enables the opening and compression of the different
      hydraulic cylinders to be regulated.

      The control board at the front has a panel and switch panel that enable the machine
      stages to be viewed.

1 2.1    OPERATION


1       LOADING
        The piston of the box drives the slide that places the material in the compression
        chamber. When the box is at the end of its stroke, i.e. completely shut, the
        pressing piston starts the compaction phase.

2       COMPACTION
        The pressing piston moves toward, compacting the material placed in the chamber
        by means of the plunger.
        The plunger forward stroke is contrasted by the action of the press tongs with
        variable pressure that are regulated by the operator, according to the type of
        material used. At the end of the plunger stroke, there is a limit switch that reverses
        the stroke to start the rapid-return phase.

3       RETURN PHASE
        The box slide and the plunger perform the return stroke simultaneously. During this
        phase, the pressing plunger, due to a lever that is connected to it. mechanically
        rotates the wheel of the disk wheel that moves and sends to the box part of the
        material contained in the material tank. At the end of the phase, the box is ready to
        start for a new cycle.

12.6   OPERATING PARAMETERS

       The machine must have the fol owing settings for correct operation:


             PRESS TONGS GAUGE
             It should show between 90 and 130 bar (recommended setting).

             PUMP GAUGE
             The gauge has been set at 150 bar, so at each pressing phase the gauge
             should read between 130 and 150 bar.


             PRESSURE SWITCH
             It must be set at 80. To increase press tongs pressure, rotate clockwise the
             setting screw located above the pressure switch. To decrease it, rotate in the
             opposite direction, i.e. anticlockwise.


             BOX OPENING
             When light material is used, the box opening should be comprised between
              10 and 20 cm.
             If a heavy material is used, the box opening should be set at 5 cm and can
             reach a maximum of 10 cm. Use a number 19 wrench (supplied by
             manufacturer) to adjust by tightening the nut located on the right-hand side of
             the briquette press.
